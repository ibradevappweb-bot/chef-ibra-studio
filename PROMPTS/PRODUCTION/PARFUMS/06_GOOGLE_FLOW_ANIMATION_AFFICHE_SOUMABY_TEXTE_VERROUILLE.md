# GOOGLE FLOW — ANIMATION DE L’AFFICHE SOUMABY (TEXTE VERROUILLÉ)

## Objectif
Transformer l’affiche carrée fournie en une courte vidéo publicitaire élégante, sans redessiner ni réinterpréter l’affiche.

## PROMPT À COLLER DANS GOOGLE FLOW

Use the supplied poster image as the exact, immutable source frame and preserve it as a single finished graphic design.

**ABSOLUTE TEXT AND LOGO LOCK:**
- Every letter, word, number, punctuation mark, accent, line break, font, font size, text position, spacing and alignment must remain EXACTLY identical to the source image in every frame.
- Do not rewrite, regenerate, redraw, reinterpret, translate, correct, complete, stylize, sharpen into different characters, or replace ANY text.
- Do not change the spelling or appearance of “L’ÉLÉGANCE A DEUX SIGNATURES”, “Découvrez deux fragrances de caractère”, “Soumaby”, “+223 92 91 12 12”, “soumaby.com”, “12726 rue 455, Hamdallaye ACI 2000, Bamako” or “CONCEPT DÉMO — IBRA BUSINESS AI”.
- Preserve the Soumaby logo exactly as shown. Do not invent or modify any logo, icon, symbol or brand mark.
- Treat all text, logos, contact details and graphic elements as a flattened, locked image layer. They are not objects to animate.

**PRODUCT LOCK:**
- Keep both perfume bottles exactly as shown in the source image.
- Do not alter bottle shape, proportions, cap, colors, glass, labels, brand names, reflections or position.
- Do not add, remove, duplicate or morph any product.

**ALLOWED MOTION ONLY:**
- Keep the camera completely fixed: no zoom, pan, tilt, rotation, reframing or perspective shift.
- Add only extremely subtle ambient light movement in the existing background, behind the poster elements: a soft blue glow on the left and a soft warm-gold glow on the right.
- If possible, add a very faint, slow background shimmer on the black marble without affecting the bottles, text, logo or contact information.
- The overall result must remain calm, premium, clean and readable.

**STRICT NEGATIVE INSTRUCTIONS:**
No new text. No subtitles. No captions. No voiceover text. No transitions that distort the poster. No moving letters. No flicker on typography. No melting, warping, morphing, spelling changes, invented characters, misspelled words, altered numbers, altered URL, altered address, altered phone number, logo redesign, bottle animation, extra objects, particles, smoke, lens flare over text, camera shake or cropping.

**Output:** 8 seconds, vertical 9:16 only if the supplied poster is already composed for vertical framing; otherwise preserve the original square 1:1 composition without cropping or stretching. The source poster must remain perfectly legible and visually unchanged throughout the clip.

If exact preservation of the source text cannot be guaranteed, do not regenerate the poster elements: keep the entire poster completely still.

## Réglage et contrôle
1. Importer la deuxième affiche choisie comme image de référence.
2. Générer un seul essai court.
3. Vérifier image par image le numéro WhatsApp, l’URL, l’adresse, le logo et tous les titres.
4. Si Flow modifie ne serait-ce qu’un caractère, ne pas utiliser le résultat final : conserver l’affiche fixe et ajouter le mouvement du fond dans un éditeur vidéo avec l’affiche placée au-dessus comme calque verrouillé.

## Note technique
Un prompt ne peut pas garantir à 100 % que Flow préservera les caractères d’une image. Pour une fidélité absolue des textes, la méthode fiable est de garder l’affiche originale comme calque fixe/verrouillé et d’animer uniquement un arrière-plan séparé.
